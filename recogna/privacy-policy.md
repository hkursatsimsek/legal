# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 8, 2026
**Last Updated:** April 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Recogna ("the App") is a vocabulary learning application for iOS developed by H. Kürşat Şimşek ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Email address** — provided during email/password registration or through Sign in with Apple
- **Display name** — optionally provided during registration or retrieved from your Apple ID
- **User ID** — a unique identifier generated by our authentication service

If you use Sign in with Apple with the "Hide My Email" option, we only receive Apple's relay email address.

### 2.2 Learning Data

As you use the App, we store the following data to provide the learning experience:

- Vocabulary words, translations, and pronunciation guides
- Categories and decks you create or install
- Context sentences associated with words
- Learning progress (mastery level, review intervals, correct/incorrect counts)
- Quiz results and scores
- Your app preferences and settings (e.g., daily goal, flashcard count, notification preferences)

### 2.3 Data Processed On-Device Only

The following data is processed entirely on your device and is **never transmitted** to any server:

- **Camera images** — used for real-time text and object recognition to help you discover new vocabulary
- **Photo library images** — used for text and object recognition when you select a photo
- **Audio recordings** — captured from the microphone for pronunciation assessment, processed on-device using Apple's Speech Recognition framework
- **Object detection results** — generated on-device using machine learning models (CoreML)
- **Text recognition results** — generated on-device using Apple's Vision framework
- **Language analysis** — performed on-device using Apple's Natural Language framework

### 2.4 Automatically Collected Data

We automatically collect certain diagnostic information to maintain app quality:

- **Crash data** — stack traces, device model, operating system version, and app version collected when the App crashes
- **Performance data** — general performance metrics to identify and resolve issues
- **Firebase installation ID** — a device-level identifier used by Firebase services for delivery and diagnostics

### 2.5 Research Data (Optional)

If you opt in to our research program, we may additionally collect:

- Learning event logs (e.g., quiz answers, flashcard interactions, camera detection events)
- Session timestamps and response times
- Study mode selections

Research participation is entirely voluntary and can be opted out of at any time.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

| Purpose | Data Used |
|---------|-----------|
| Provide the vocabulary learning experience | Learning data, preferences |
| Authenticate your account | Email, name, user ID |
| Sync your data across devices | Learning data, preferences |
| Assess your pronunciation | Microphone audio (on-device only) |
| Recognize text and objects from images | Camera/photo data (on-device only) |
| Display mnemonic images for vocabulary | Search queries sent to Pexels API |
| Diagnose crashes and improve stability | Crash data, performance data |
| Configure app features remotely | Device country, language, OS version, app version |
| Conduct optional learning research | Research event data (if opted in) |

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App's core functionality.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- **Local storage**: Your learning data is stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.
- **Cloud storage**: If you create an account, your data is synced to Google Cloud Firestore, encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest.
- **Mnemonic images**: Downloaded images are stored locally on your device.
- **Temporary files**: Audio recordings for pronunciation assessment are stored temporarily and deleted after processing.
- **No plaintext transmission**: All network communication uses HTTPS encryption.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## 6. Data Sharing

- We do **not sell** your personal information to any third party.
- We do **not share** your data for advertising or marketing purposes.
- We do **not** use your data for cross-app or cross-site tracking.
- We share data with third parties **only** as described in Section 4, solely to provide the App's core functionality.
- We may disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or governmental request.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

### 7.1 Data Retention

- **Local data**: Retained on your device until you delete it manually or uninstall the App.
- **Cloud data**: Retained in your Firebase account as long as your account exists.
- **Crash logs**: Retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
- **Research data**: Retained locally on your device until you export or delete it.

### 7.2 Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly within the App (Profile > Delete Account). When you delete your account:

1. All your data stored in Cloud Firestore (words, categories, progress, quiz results, settings) is permanently deleted.
2. Your Firebase Authentication record is deleted.
3. If you signed in with Apple, your Apple Sign in credentials are revoked.
4. Local data on your device remains until you uninstall the App or delete it manually.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We aim to complete all deletion within 30 days of your request.

### 7.3 Data Export

You can export your learning data (words, categories, progress, quiz results) in JSON or CSV format from within the App at any time.

## 9. Device Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions. Each is optional and you can deny or revoke access at any time through iOS Settings:

| Permission | Why We Need It |
|------------|----------------|
| **Camera** | To recognize text and objects in real-time for vocabulary discovery |
| **Microphone** | To capture your pronunciation for assessment |
| **Photo Library** | To recognize text and objects in images you select |
| **Speech Recognition** | To evaluate your pronunciation accuracy |
| **Notifications** | To send daily learning reminders (if enabled) |

The App remains functional without these permissions. For example, you can add words manually without using the camera, and you can learn vocabulary without using pronunciation features.

## 10. On-Device Processing

We are committed to processing your data on-device whenever possible. The following features operate entirely on your device with **no data transmitted to external servers**:

- **Object detection**: Uses CoreML models running locally on your device
- **Text recognition**: Uses Apple's Vision framework on-device
- **Speech recognition**: Uses Apple's Speech framework with on-device processing (iOS 17+)
- **Text-to-speech**: Uses Apple's AVFoundation framework locally
- **Language detection and analysis**: Uses Apple's Natural Language framework on-device
- **Translation**: Uses Apple's built-in translation capabilities on-device
- **Word embeddings**: Uses Apple's Natural Language word vectors stored on-device
